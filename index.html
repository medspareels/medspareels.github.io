Before you start

Are the reels made with AI?

Yes. Visuals are AI-made or from your own photos and clips, and AI visuals are labelled on screen. The voice is an AI voice. Nothing shows a fake patient, provider or result.

Are they compliant?

We avoid promised results, fake before-and-afters, superlatives and misuse of brand names, and add disclaimers and your medical director line. Rules differ by state, so your medical director should approve each month before you post.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, colors, Instagram handle, treatment menu and your medical director's name. About 5 minutes.

When does each month arrive?

Before the month starts, so you can review and schedule.
